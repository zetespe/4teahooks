4tea Hooks

Keep your place in any crochet pattern, even when you pick it up again a week later.

Open the app View the source

What it does

Any pattern, turned into rows to tick

Toys, garments, blankets, granny squares. Your chatbot (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini…) converts the pattern for the size you're making; the app splits it into parts and rows.

Never lose your place

Tick rounds as you go. Parts you make twice (ears, sleeves) are tracked separately. Stopped mid-row? Leave a note right on it.

What was that stitch again?

Tap any stitch in a row to see how it's made, with the US/UK name, because the same name means different stitches in US and UK patterns.

Several projects at once

Each project opens where you left off, with a link back to the original pattern for photos and charts.

Your projects stay with you

4tea Hooks runs entirely on your device. Your patterns, progress and notes are stored in the browser's local storage. There is no account, no cloud and no server that could see your data. The chatbot conversion happens in the chatbot you choose, by copy and paste: you decide what you share.

Your data exists in one place, so save a backup file now and then (Backup & settings). Keep it in Files, Google Drive, email, wherever you like, and restore it on any device.

What is counted, and only this: to know whether anyone uses the app, visits to this page are counted, and the app sends one anonymous “opened today / this week / this month” signal plus a single “first use” signal. They go to GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter without cookies. They carry no ID and none of your patterns or progress. Like any website visit, GoatCounter also notes the browser, system and country with each count (and, for visits to this page, the screen width and the page that linked here), but not your IP address. The app's counts are never linked to each other.

Install it
